Distribution Architecture, Source Availability & GPL Compliance
===============================================================
Ratex splits distribution artifacts into two distinct deterministic archives:

1. Runtime Payload (`packages.tar.zst`, sharded as `packages.tar.zst.00`..`05`):
   Contains all pre-compiled font outlines (Type 1 PFB, OpenType OTF, and
   TrueType TTF), font metrics (TFM), virtual fonts (VF), encodings (ENC), font
   definitions (FD), and mapping files (MAP) required for offline typesetting.
   Raw development sources are excluded from the runtime binary payload.
   Exact compressed and uncompressed sizes, member counts, and SHA256 hashes
   are recorded in packages.lock.json alongside the distribution.

2. Corresponding Source Archive (`sources.tar.zst`):
   Under the GNU General Public License v2.0 (GPLv2 Section 3) and related
   licenses, distributing binaries or embedded font programs requires providing
   the complete corresponding machine-readable source code. Pointing to external
   URLs or hashes alone does not satisfy this legal obligation.

   Therefore, Ratex packages and distributes the complete, verbatim corresponding
   source archives alongside binary releases in `sources.tar.zst`:
   - `cm-super.zip` (67,319,801 bytes, SHA256: f3f856a5006ae7d2bbc2d9a338ca5ae25b6fba19eebee3ba20892845aafa7987):
     Upstream CTAN package by Vladimir Volovich with Type 1 PFB outlines, AFM
