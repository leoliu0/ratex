\section*{Typography and typesetting systems}
Early work by \citet{knuth84} established the machinery that later authors
extended \citep{lamport94,beeton07}. The font collection described in
\citep[see][p.~7]{gyre12} came much later. \citeauthor{lamport94} wrote of structure,
while \citeyear{knuth84} marks the first edition. Multiple groups agree
\citep{knuth84,lamport94}; compare \citealp{gyre12}.
